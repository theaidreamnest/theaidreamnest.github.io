Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026 · The AI Dream Nest

This Privacy Policy explains how the The AI Dream Nest application ("the App", "we", "us") handles data. The App is a private, personal automation tool that publishes original content to the owner's own social media accounts (including Instagram and TikTok) using those platforms' official APIs. The App is operated by its owner for their own accounts only; it does not offer services to, or collect data from, any other users.

1. Information we access

The App accesses only what is required to publish content to the owner's own accounts:

  • Authentication tokens — OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by the connected platforms (Instagram/Meta, TikTok) for the owner's own account. These authorize the App to publish on the owner's behalf.
  • Account identifiers — the owner's own account/user ID returned by the platform, used to target the correct account when publishing.
  • Content created by the owner — the images, videos, and captions the App generates and uploads.

The App does not collect, request, buy, or process personal data about any other people, followers, or third parties. It does not track visitors, use advertising identifiers, or build user profiles.

2. How information is used

Access tokens and account identifiers are used solely to publish the owner's own content to the owner's own accounts through the official platform APIs. Generated content is uploaded to the connected platforms for publishing. Data is not used for any other purpose.

3. How information is stored

Authentication tokens are stored as encrypted secrets within the owner's own automation environment (for example, repository/CI secrets) and, where a refresh token must persist between runs, in the owner's own private storage. Tokens are transmitted only to the issuing platform's official API endpoints over secure (HTTPS) connections.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. The only external services contacted are the official APIs of the connected platforms (Instagram/Meta, TikTok) and the media hosting used to deliver the owner's own content to those platforms for publishing. No data is shared with anyone else.

5. Data retention and revocation

Tokens are retained only while the App is in use and are refreshed or replaced as required by the platforms. The owner can revoke the App's access at any time from the connected platform's app/security settings, which immediately ends the App's ability to access the account. Stored tokens can be deleted by the owner at any time.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly process any data relating to children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling, contact: theaidreamnest@gmail.com.
